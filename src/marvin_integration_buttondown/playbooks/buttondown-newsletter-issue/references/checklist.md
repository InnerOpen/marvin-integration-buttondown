# Before you hand an issue back

## Fields
- [ ] The title reads well as a subject on its own, about 60 characters or fewer
- [ ] The preview is one sentence, about 140 characters or fewer, and does not repeat the title
- [ ] The body is Markdown only, with no header, greeting banner, footer or unsubscribe line

## Content
- [ ] Every fact comes from the entries or the person's brief; nothing is invented (dates, prices, numbers, quotes)
- [ ] It opens with the point
- [ ] It can be read in about two minutes
- [ ] One thing to do at most (a link to read more, a reply, a date). Not three

## Links and images
- [ ] Every link is a real address, taken from the tools, and goes where its text says
- [ ] Every image has a full public address and alt text
- [ ] No link points at a draft or a page that is not published

## Handing it back
- [ ] Saved with `create_draft` or `update_draft`, not published
- [ ] Your reply has the review link and says it sends (or becomes a Buttondown draft) when someone publishes it
- [ ] Anything you guessed at is named in your reply
