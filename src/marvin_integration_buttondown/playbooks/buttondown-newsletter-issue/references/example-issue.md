# An example issue

This shows the shape of a finished issue, not wording to copy.

**Title (subject):** Three changes to how we ship orders

**Preview:** Faster dispatch, tracking by default, and one thing we stopped doing.

**Body:**

```markdown
Orders now leave within two working days instead of five. Here is what changed behind that.

## What is different

- **Dispatch:** packed and posted on Tuesdays and Fridays.
- **Tracking:** every parcel has a tracking link, emailed when it leaves.
- **Gift notes:** we no longer print them, because they arrived creased. Write one on the order and we will tuck in a card.

## Why

The slow part was waiting for a courier slot, not packing. Booking two fixed collections a week removed the wait.

If something you ordered has not arrived, reply to this email and we will look into it.

[Read the full shipping page](https://example.com/shipping/)
```

Why it works: the subject says what is inside, the preview adds detail instead of repeating it, the body opens with the
point, uses one short list, ends with one link, and has no header or footer of its own.
